# ProjectOne — 04 User Personas (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

Define the primary user personas that ProjectOne is designed to serve.

## Persona 1 – Solo Creator

Runs one or more content channels alone. Wants to save time, automate repetitive work and publish consistently without hiring a team.

### Goals

Increase output, maintain quality, reduce editing time, grow audience and revenue.

### Challenges

Too many tools, repetitive tasks, limited time and inconsistent workflows.

## Persona 2 – Content Business

A small company or entrepreneur producing content as part of a larger business.

### Goals

Standardize production, automate processes and improve collaboration while keeping quality high.

### Challenges

Scaling production, maintaining consistency and reducing operational costs.

## Persona 3 – Small Creator Team

A team of creators, editors and managers working together on multiple channels.

### Goals

Centralize workflows, coordinate production and simplify approvals.

### Challenges

Communication, version control and fragmented tools.

## Shared Characteristics

All personas value time, consistency, transparency, automation, quality and long-term business growth over short-term experimentation.
