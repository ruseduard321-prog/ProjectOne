# ProjectOne — 03 Target Audience (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

Define exactly who ProjectOne is built for and who it is not built for.

## Primary Audience

Independent content creators who publish consistently and want to automate repetitive work while maintaining creative control.

## Secondary Audience

Small creator teams, agencies, entrepreneurs, educators and businesses that produce content regularly.

## Ideal Customer

A creator who treats content as a business, values efficiency, is willing to invest in tools that save time, and wants one platform instead of many disconnected applications.

## Customer Goals

Produce more content, improve quality, save time, increase consistency, grow audience and revenue, and simplify daily workflows.

## Pain Points

Too many tools, repetitive manual work, context switching, inconsistent workflows, slow production, complex AI ecosystems and fragmented data.

## Who ProjectOne Is NOT For

Casual users who rarely create content, users looking only for a free AI chatbot, people unwilling to learn a professional workflow, or users expecting AI to replace all creative decisions.

## Target Outcome

Enable creators to spend less time managing tools and more time building valuable content businesses.
