# ProjectOne — 02 Product Principles (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

This document defines the non-negotiable principles that guide every product decision in ProjectOne.

## Principle 1 – Solve Problems or Save Time

Every feature must answer at least one question: What problem does it solve? How much time does it save? If the answer to both is 'almost none', the feature should not be built.

## Principle 2 – User Control

Automation should never remove user control. AI recommends and executes when allowed, but the user always has the final decision.

## Principle 3 – Simplicity First

The simplest solution that achieves the goal is preferred over a more complex one.

## Principle 4 – Quality Over Quantity

One excellent workflow is more valuable than multiple average workflows.

## Principle 5 – Connected Experience

Every feature should feel like part of one operating system, not a collection of separate tools.

## Principle 6 – Transparency

Costs, AI providers, limitations, failures and important decisions must always be visible to the user.

## Principle 7 – AI With Purpose

AI exists to remove repetitive work, improve decisions and increase productivity—not to be present in every screen.

## Principle 8 – Modular by Design

Every major system should be replaceable without requiring a complete redesign of the platform.

## Principle 9 – Trust

Protect user data, respect privacy and avoid dark patterns or misleading behavior.

## Principle 10 – Long-Term Thinking

Every product decision should improve ProjectOne not only for today's users but also for future growth.
