# ProjectOne — 16 Billing (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

The Billing module manages subscriptions, payments, usage, invoices and account limits in a transparent and predictable way.

## Objectives

Provide users with complete visibility over plans, costs, AI usage and subscription status while making upgrades and payments simple.

## Core Capabilities

Subscription management, monthly and yearly plans, invoice history, payment methods, usage tracking, plan comparison, upgrades, downgrades and cancellation.

## Usage & Limits

Display AI consumption, generated minutes, storage usage, API usage, plan limits, warnings and estimated upcoming charges before limits are reached.

See also: [[AI Providers]]

## Design Principles

Transparent pricing, no hidden costs, predictable billing, clear upgrade paths and user control over spending.

## Success Criteria

Users always understand what they are paying for, what they have used and what will happen before additional charges occur.
