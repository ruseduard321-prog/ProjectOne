# ProjectOne — 15 Analytics (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

The Analytics module transforms raw performance data into actionable insights that help creators improve content quality, consistency and business growth.

## Objectives

Provide clear metrics, identify trends, measure AI performance, estimate ROI and recommend improvements based on historical data.

## Core Metrics

Views, watch time, engagement, subscribers, publishing consistency, AI generation costs, estimated revenue, profit estimation, workflow duration and content performance.

## AI Insights

Identify successful patterns, detect weak-performing content, recommend optimizations, compare strategies and learn from previous results.

## Design Principles

Actionable over decorative, simple over complex, explain every recommendation, and prioritize business impact.

## Success Criteria

Users understand what is working, what is not, and what they should do next without manually analyzing large amounts of data.
