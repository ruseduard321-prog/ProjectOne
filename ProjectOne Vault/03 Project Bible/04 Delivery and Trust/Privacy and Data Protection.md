# ProjectOne — Privacy & Data Protection (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

Describe how ProjectOne collects, stores, processes and deletes user data responsibly.

## Data Ownership

Users retain ownership of their data and can export or permanently delete it.

## Privacy Principles

Collect the minimum necessary data, provide transparency and never sell user data.

## Data Retention

Retention periods are documented, justified and enforced automatically where possible.

## Success Criteria

Privacy is embedded into every feature by design.
