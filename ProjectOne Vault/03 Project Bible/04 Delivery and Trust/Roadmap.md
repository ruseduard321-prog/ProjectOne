# ProjectOne — 40 Roadmap (Draft v0.1)

## Purpose

Define the long-term evolution of ProjectOne and provide a structured development direction without prescribing implementation details.

## Phase 1 – Foundation

Core platform, authentication, workspaces, AI providers, projects, generation pipeline and essential workflows.

## Phase 2 – Productivity

Advanced AI agents, analytics, automation, memory improvements, publishing and optimization features.

## Phase 3 – Scale

Team collaboration, enterprise capabilities, marketplace, advanced integrations and expanded AI orchestration.

## Planning Principles

Prioritize user value, measurable time savings, platform stability and architectural quality over feature quantity.

## Success Criteria

Every release moves ProjectOne closer to becoming the default operating system for content businesses.
