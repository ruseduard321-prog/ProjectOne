# ProjectOne Engineering Handbook

## Chapter 8 — AI Engineering Standards

### Objective

AI systems must be deterministic where possible and observable.

### Prompts

Version every system prompt and document changes.

### Memory

Persist only valuable long-term information.

See also: [[Memory System]]

### Model Selection

Choose models by capability, latency and cost.

See also: [[AI Providers]]

### Fallbacks

Critical workflows require provider fallback.

### Summary

AI remains reliable, affordable and explainable.
